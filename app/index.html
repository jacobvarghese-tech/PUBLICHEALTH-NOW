Congressional App Challenge 2026

Pattern Check

Pattern Check ranks Medicare billers whose public billing for a watched service is unusually concentrated, fast-growing, or high-charged compared with other billers of the same code.

For students, reporters, and anyone reading a public Medicare file. A high score is a resemblance to an unusual pattern. It is not a fraud finding.

How to read a row

Pick a service and a year. The table uses only the public Medicare Physician & Other Practitioners file. No patient-level claims are in this app.

Video example: epilepsy gene panel, code 81419

Prince Laboratories and Reliable Results, the Deerfield Beach labs in the October 2026 Coffeezilla report, billed this code in 2023 and 2024. Open 2024 and search either name. The ranking puts both near the top on national share and on the share coming out of ZIP 33441. Neither lab is on the October 15, 2026 exclusion list. A high rank here is a pattern match, not a sanction.

Scores are computed in the browser from data/rankings.json. Source notes and the label count are on the about page.
