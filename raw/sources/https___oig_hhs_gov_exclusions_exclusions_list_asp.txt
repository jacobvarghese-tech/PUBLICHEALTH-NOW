                            <h1>LEIE Database &amp; Supplement Downloads</h1>
                        
                        
                            



    
    
        <p class="usa-intro" data-block-key="lodwr">HHS-OIG offers a CSV download of active OIG exclusions that can be used on your local computer or to populate the database program of your choice.</p>
    

    
    
        

<div class="pep-alert usa-alert usa-alert--success" >
    <div class="usa-alert__body">
        
            <h3 class="usa-alert__heading">10-09-2026</h3>
        
        
            <p data-block-key="e13ss">Last Update</p>
        
    </div>
</div>

    

    
    
        <p data-block-key="3h9r9"></p><h2 data-block-key="7ffe4" id="working-with-the-leie-files"><b>Working with the LEIE files</b></h2><p data-block-key="7gqm2">It is important to understand the nature of the LEIE files and decide the best strategy for your organization. We suggest reviewing the <a href="/exclusions/leie-quick-tips-instructions/">Getting Started</a> section, and then doing the following:</p><ol><li data-block-key="2s7la">Download the <a href="#leie-database-download">LEIE Database</a> to setup your local system for searches.</li><li data-block-key="aft3e"><b>Pick one of the following strategies each month</b> to keep your local database up-to-date. Either,<br/><b>•</b> Download the <a href="#leie-database-download">latest full database file</a>, OR<br/><b>•</b> Append your information by applying the <a href="#latest-monthly-supplements">monthly supplement files</a>.</li></ol><p data-block-key="aem3t">Please read the <a href="/exclusions/leie-quick-tips-instructions/">LEIE Quick Tips &amp; Instructions</a> if you are new to the LEIE or you wish to improve the efficiency of your searches.</p>
    

    
    
        

<div class="pep-alert usa-alert usa-alert--warning usa-alert--slim" >
    <div class="usa-alert__body">
        
        
            <p data-block-key="nzhlr">The Privacy Act prohibits the distribution of SSNs so regardless of your exact process, you'll need to use the <a href="https://exclusions.oig.hhs.gov/">Online Search</a> to verify specific individuals and entities.</p>
        
    </div>
</div>

    

    
    
        <p data-block-key="m7fi6"></p><h2 data-block-key="s017" id="leie-database-download"><b>LEIE Database Download</b></h2><p data-block-key="6eob2">The LEIE Database CSV file contains<b> the entire dataset of all exclusions in effect</b>. Every month, this file is replaced with the most current version of the complete database.</p><ul><li data-block-key="cdc1l">Individuals and entities who have been reinstated<b> are removed</b> from this file.</li><li data-block-key="3fjno"><b>If you download the LEIE database each month,</b> do NOT use the Monthly Supplement Files.<ul><li data-block-key="1rl10"><a href="https://oig.hhs.gov/exclusions/downloadables/UPDATED.csv">09-2026 Updated LEIE Database</a> (CSV)</li></ul></li></ul><p data-block-key="blak6"><a href="/documents/root/10419/Current%20LEIE%20Database%20Record%20Layout.pdf">Database Record Layout</a> (PDF) - Defines the different fields contained within the LEIE Downloadable Database and how many characters are in each field.</p>
    

    
    
        

<div class="usa-accordion margin-top-6">
    



    
    
        

<h2 id="why-does-the-last-update-date-not-match-the-date-on-leie-filenames" class="usa-accordion__heading" >
    <button class="usa-accordion__button" aria-controls="acc-why-does-the-last-update-date-not-match-the-date-on-leie-filenames">
        Why does the Last Update date not match the date on LEIE filenames?
    </button>
</h2>
<div id="acc-why-does-the-last-update-date-not-match-the-date-on-leie-filenames" class="usa-accordion__content usa-prose">
    



    
    
        <p data-block-key="paly8">LEIE files include <b>all actions taken during the prior month</b>. For example, if the update date is December, the Database Download file contains all exclusions through the end of November. The Supplement files contains exclusions and reinstatement that occurred during the month of November.</p>
    


</div>

    


</div>
    

    
    
        <h2 data-block-key="3h9r9" id="latest-monthly-supplements"><b>Latest Monthly Supplements</b></h2><p data-block-key="cqcq3">When maintaining a local version of the database, you can use these files to <b>manually</b> <b>add new exclusions</b> and <b>remove reinstated entities</b> to update your database so it reflects the status as of the month indicated in the filename.</p><p data-block-key="5d53a"></p>
    

    
    
        

<div class="pep-alert usa-alert usa-alert--info usa-alert--slim" >
    <div class="usa-alert__body">
        
        
            <p data-block-key="hi01y">For the most accurate and complete data, OIG recommends downloading the updated <a href="#leie-database-download">LEIE Database</a> rather than relying solely on these monthly incremental files. Supplement files contain actions for a single month only – they do NOT include the full database.</p>
        
    </div>
</div>

    

    
    
        <h3 data-block-key="7x9bl" id="september-2026">September 2026</h3><ul><li data-block-key="72cfi"><a href="https://oig.hhs.gov/exclusions/downloadables/2026/2609excl.csv">09-2026 Exclusions</a> (CSV)</li><li data-block-key="de1g8"><a href="https://oig.hhs.gov/exclusions/downloadables/2026/2609rein.csv">09-2026 Reinstatements</a> (CSV)</li></ul><h2 data-block-key="jhhn" id="profile-corrections">Profile Corrections</h2><p data-block-key="9tn72">Changes made to the excluded individual or entity's profile, such as a change of address, etc. Do NOT use this file to verify exclusions.</p><p data-block-key="d2dtu"><a href="https://oig.hhs.gov/exclusions/files/leie_updated_information.pdf">09-2026 Profile Corrections</a> (PDF)</p><h2 data-block-key="9oopk" id="supplements-archive"><b>Supplements Archive</b></h2>
    

    
    
        <p data-block-key="7x9bl">OIG only archives monthly supplement files for the <b>previous 12 months</b>. Older information is not available on the website or via phone call to Exclusions staff although the <a href="#leie-database-download">Downloadable LEIE</a> gives users the latest information on excluded individuals or entities.</p>
