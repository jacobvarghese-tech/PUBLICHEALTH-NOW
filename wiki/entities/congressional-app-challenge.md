# Congressional App Challenge

The contest this vault exists to enter. Official site: https://www.congressionalappchallenge.us/

A free, district-by-district coding contest for U.S. middle and high school students. Each participating House member hosts a local competition. There is no national assigned topic and no required language.

Started from House Resolution 77 in 2013 (passed 411–3). First official challenge co-chairs were Reps. Mimi Walters and Hakeem Jeffries. Current co-chairs for the 119th Congress: Rep. Brett Guthrie (R-KY-02) and Rep. Kim Schrier (D-WA-08).

The June 1, 2026 launch post said more than 85,000 students had participated since 2015. The about page's older line said over 80,000 in the first eleven years. Use the launch-post figure.

2026 presenting sponsor: Roblox. Other sponsors named on the homepage in the brief: Broadcom Foundation, Qualcomm, Craig Newmark Philanthropies, Infosys Foundation USA, AWS, Meta, Comcast/NBCUniversal, theCoderSchool, and CGI.

Mission, from the about page: inspire students to try computer science, include students underrepresented in tech, and connect members to emerging technology through their own students.

Operated by the [[Internet Education Foundation]] for the [[House of Representatives]].
