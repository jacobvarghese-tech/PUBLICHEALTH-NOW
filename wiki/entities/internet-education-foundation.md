# Internet Education Foundation

Nonprofit that administers the [[Congressional App Challenge]] for the House. The rules name it as the Program Administrator, Washington, DC 20005.

The June 1, 2026 launch announcement names Joe Alessi as executive director.

Congressional operations contact recorded in the brief: cad@congressionalappchallenge.us

The foundation may distribute additional sponsor prizes equally across participating districts. It also receives the license described on [[prizes]].

Privacy policy: http://www.congressionalappchallenge.us/privacy-policy/
