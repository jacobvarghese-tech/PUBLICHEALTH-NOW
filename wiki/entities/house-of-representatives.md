# House of Representatives

Host of the [[Congressional App Challenge]]. Each participating member runs the contest for their own district and picks the winner. The national team does not announce a district result before the January 15, 2027 embargo, and result questions go to the office, not to the [[Internet Education Foundation]].

Find a member: https://www.house.gov/representatives/find-your-representative

Participating districts: https://www.congressionalappchallenge.us/students/participating-districts/

The student's member is not known yet. Do not create a member page until an address has been looked up.

Co-chairs of the competition for the 119th Congress: Brett Guthrie (KY-02) and Kim Schrier (WA-08). They are co-chairs of the program, not automatically the student's hosting office.
