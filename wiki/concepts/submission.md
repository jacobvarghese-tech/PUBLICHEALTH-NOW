# Submission

Portal: https://webportalapp.com/sp/congressional_app_challenge_2026

Register with a personal email, not a school email. The portal also wants home and school addresses with 9-digit ZIPs, the district and member, and a parent or guardian name and email. A teacher or mentor is optional. Teams: one person creates the profile and invites up to three others. Each person completes an eligibility quiz.

## Package

1. A working app created after October 30, 2025. See [[app idea]].
2. A public YouTube or Vimeo demo, 1–3 minutes. Outside that length, judges may penalize it. This is not a video contest.
3. Written answers.
4. An exit questionnaire after the deadline, filled out separately by every teammate.

The video has to include every participant's name, the app name, the purpose in one sentence, the audience, the tools and languages, and the app working.

Typical written questions from the rules:

1. Title.
2. Purpose.
3. Inspiration.
4. A technical difficulty and how you solved it.
5. What you learned.
6. What you would change in a 2.0.

Disclose libraries and any AI use in those answers. The rules require that disclosure. See [[eligibility]] for the AI limit: it can support parts of the build and cannot be the whole technical development.
