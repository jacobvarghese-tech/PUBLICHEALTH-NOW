# Judging

Each hosting office reviews the demo and the written answers. They may use a panel. The rules list three criteria:

1. Quality of the idea, including creativity and originality.
2. Implementation, including user experience and design.
3. Demonstrated coding skill.

The member can change the process. Judging is supposed to be fair and is left to that office. Odds depend on how many eligible apps arrive and how good they are.

Judges may ask to see the app and the source code. Refusing disqualifies the entry.

Offices are told to use an odd number of judges, with basic programming familiarity and no conflict of interest. Sample rubric: https://www.congressionalappchallenge.us/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/CAC-Judging-Rubric.pdf

One recognized winner per hosting district. There is no national ranking in the rules.

A local office may set a theme. If it does, [[app idea]] has to follow it.
