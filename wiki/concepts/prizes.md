# Prizes

There is no standard national cash prize. Do not plan the app around prize money.

Confirmed recognition for a district winner:

1. The app is eligible to be displayed in the U.S. Capitol. The about page says up to one year. "Eligible" is not a guarantee.
2. The app is eligible to be featured on House.gov.
3. An invitation to #HouseOfCode.
4. A waived copyright registration fee under the ARTS Act: https://copyright.gov/arts-act/
5. A press release on the contest site.
6. Extra sponsor prizes were still TBA on the office FAQ.

A member's office may give nominal awards such as certificates. A local group may give a prize of $50 or less. Anything over $50 from one source has to go through the national sponsor and be offered to winners nationwide.

#HouseOfCode for this cycle is spring 2027 at the Capitol. Winners demo their apps and meet their representative. The student pays for travel and lodging. House offices cannot use official funds for student travel.

The student keeps the intellectual property and grants Congress and the [[Internet Education Foundation]] a royalty-free license to post the submission, make winners available during the competition and for one month after the submission period, and use submissions for noncommercial purposes after that.
