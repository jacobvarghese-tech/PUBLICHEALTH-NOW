# Deadline

Plan to submit before **12:00 p.m. Eastern on Monday, October 26, 2026**.

That clock is the one in [[2026 CAC Rules PDF]]. The [[2026 contest brief]] also records the student registration page, updated 2026-10-12, saying **9:00 p.m. ET** the same day. Those two official sources disagree. Noon is the safe cutoff. After the submission period ends, the entry cannot be changed.

## Calendar

- January 1, 2026: pre-registration opened.
- May 1, 2026: challenge period start, per the rules page and the participating-districts page.
- June 1, 2026: portal launch announcement. More than 340 members had signed up.
- October 26, 2026: register and submit.
- November 2026: judging.
- December 2026: district winners announced by each office.
- January 15, 2027: embargo. The national team cannot disclose results before this date.
- Spring 2027: [[prizes|#HouseOfCode]] for this cycle's winners.

The students landing page still describes the finished 2025 window (May 1–October 30, 2025). Ignore it.
