# Eligibility

All of these have to be true at submission. Source: [[2026 CAC Rules PDF]], summarized in [[2026 contest brief]].

- Enrolled in middle school or high school on October 26, 2026. Middle school usually means grades 6–8 and varies by region. A student who graduates before the deadline is out.
- U.S. resident. Citizenship is not required.
- Competing in a district that is hosting, and in only one district: home or school.
- Individual, or a team of at most 4. At least half the team must live or attend school in the district being entered.
- One app per person per year. No second team entry.
- App created after October 30, 2025. A 2.0 of an older app is allowed only if the new coding is what gets judged.

The participating-district list in the brief was current on the site as of 2026-10-05. Re-check it before locking a district. Most districts were hosting. Some were blank. Do not assume a blank district will sign up before [[deadline]].

This vault does not yet know the student's district. Do not invent one.
