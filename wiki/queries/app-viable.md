# Is the app viable

## Question

Is Medicare Fraud Flagger viable?

## Answer

Yes, as a working lookup on this laptop. The two public files exist, the join rule is published, and one shallow model on eight numbers can train after the years are rolled in chunks.

It is viable as a review flag. A high score means the billing looks like providers who were later excluded. It does not prove fraud. How often the 2024 exclusions land in the top 1% and top 5% is unknown until step 6 of [[action plan]]. If that capture is weak, the page prints the number and still ships.
